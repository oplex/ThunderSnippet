one Tab away

Write the same email once

Order confirmations, quotes, follow-ups, support answers: type a short key in the message and press Tab, or search all your snippets with Alt+Shift+S.

⇥

Key + Tab

Type ty and press Tab: the key becomes the whole text, right where you are typing. Space works too, if you prefer.

✎

Fields

{{PONUMBER}} opens a small form with a live preview. Defaults, choices, and last time's values filled in.

👤

Knows who you write to

{{to.first}}, {{me.name}}, {{subject}}, dates in any format, and where the caret ends up.

📎

Attachments

Snippets can bring their files, named from the fields: PO {{PONUMBER}} terms.pdf becomes PO 4711 terms.pdf.

✉️

Subject and recipients

A snippet can put [PO {{PONUMBER}}] before the subject, add your manager on Cc, replace To, set Reply-To or an X- header.

🧩

Snippets in snippets

{{snip:sig}} puts your signature snippet in; its fields are asked in the same form.

Everything a snippet can contain

{{PONUMBER}}A field you fill in ({{PONUMBER|12345}} with a default, {{GREETING|Hi|Hello}} as a choice)
{{to.first}} {{to.name}} {{to.email}}The first recipient ({{to.first|there}} when the name is unknown)
{{me.name}} {{me.email}}You
{{subject}}The subject
{{date}} {{date:YYYY-MM-DD}} {{time}}Today, in your format or any pattern
{{attachments}}The names of the message's attachments, kept up to date when you add or remove files later
{{snip:sig}}Another snippet
{{cursor}}Where the caret goes after inserting

Install

  1. Download the .xpi for your Thunderbird from the latest release (one for 140 and newer, one for 91–139).
  2. In Thunderbird: Add-ons and Themes → ⚙ → Install Add-on From File… Updates install automatically after that.
  3. Write a message, type po1 and press Tab. Example snippets are already there to try and change.
